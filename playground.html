bubble wand

Turns your cursor into a bubble wand. Move, scroll or click to blow bubbles, and click a bubble to pop it. When the liquid meter runs low, refill at the pool in the bottom right. Pop 10 and something happens.

The bubble wand needs a bigger screen. Try it on a desktop or tablet.

keyboard: b blows a bubble · p pops one · r refills · esc turns the wand off

shader demo

A live WebGPU shader called "Sapphire Glow Up". Move your cursor over it and the glow follows. Randomize the colors, or open fine tune to adjust it. It's built with the shaders npm package, loaded straight from a CDN with no bundler. Needs Chrome or Edge 113+, or Safari 18+.
